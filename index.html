Explore the coast at your own pace. Hitting pedestrians or police cars, or stealing any nearby vehicle, starts a chase and earns wanted stars. More stars send more cruisers; four stars bring police helicopters. Cruisers can only ram your car; cops arrest you only after you bail out, and you can mash A/D or W/S to resist for a few seconds. Stay out of sight for three minutes to lose the heat, or duck into a teal-marked safe house. Roving gangs rob people and steal cars; the air-defense battery near the river shoots down hostile drones. The first few crashes only damage the car; later impacts make it smoke heavily, catch fire and explode — only that explosion can waste you. Press R to reset or E to bail out. Stop at a restaurant pickup window to start a food delivery gig, follow the GPS marker to the numbered home, then stop at the porch to hand the order to the resident and collect the fare. Teen motorcycle crews tear donuts through parks and lawns by day; after dark they burglarize homes and steal cars, and cruisers chase them until they are caught. Press Q or Esc from gameplay to return to the main menu. Downloaded character models are credited in ATTRIBUTION.md.
