PSX South Asian Villagers - FREE Sample v1.0
by LowPoly Lanka - https://lowpolylanka.itch.io

Two rigged and animated PS1-style villagers from the PSX South Asian
Villagers pack, 3 looks each, with all 22 animations:

Tuk-tuk driver   shirt, trousers, towel over the shoulder   812 tris
Achchi           grandmother in a sari, grey bun, glasses   965 tris

Models/<Person>/   One FBX and one GLB per look (_A, _B, _C), plus PNG
                   textures. The GLB has the textures embedded.

ANIMATIONS (24 fps, all in place)
Idle, IdleShift, LookAround, PhoneCall, Walk, Run, Flee, BackAway,
TurnLeft, TurnRight, Talk, Listen, Nod, HeadWobble, Wave, Point,
SitDown, Sit, StandUp, Startle, Cower, Death

NOTES
- TurnLeft and TurnRight end turned 90 degrees. Rotate the root when they end.
- Sit: seat 0.45 m high (0.41 m for the Achchi), centre 0.10 m behind the origin.
- The phone and the driver's towel are separate meshes you can hide.

SPECS
- 19 bones, one shared skeleton, rigid skinning like real PS1 games
- Bone names: Hips, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, Left/Right Shoulder, UpperArm,
  LowerArm, Hand, UpperLeg, LowerLeg, Foot
- Pixel-art textures, 8 to 64 px. Set filtering to Point / Nearest.
- Real-world metre scale, origin at the feet

and a schoolgirl, plus editable .blend files:
